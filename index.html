⚡ Event-driven processing

Business events can be produced and consumed independently, allowing services to scale without tightly coupling every workflow.

☁ Cloud-native runtime

Containerized workloads are orchestrated through Kubernetes on Azure AKS for resilient service operation and operational control.

🔎 Operational intelligence

Elasticsearch supports investigation and search while Redis provides a fast cache layer for frequently accessed operational state.

📊 Observable by design

Telemetry and service metrics flow into monitoring views so operators can investigate latency, errors, throughput and infrastructure health.
